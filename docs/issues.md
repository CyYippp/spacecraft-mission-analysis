# Engineering issue log

No open engineering issues are recorded at this repository-setup milestone.
Record a genuine defect or unresolved discrepancy here when encountered.
Use one entry per issue with:

- ID and date
- Trigger and inputs
- Expected result and actual result
- Consequence
- Root-cause evidence
- Disposition or bounded limitation
- Retest evidence
- Status

A failed check stays visible until corrected and retested, or explicitly
accepted with its limitation.
